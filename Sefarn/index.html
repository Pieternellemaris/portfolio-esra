Title:

cvǔ blendyn rhon sěfarn lēkryt jěnǎ fōqon hyl kjǎr –

Translation:

On the eastern plains fires burnt through skin and soul.

Date:

Autumn 2024

Size:

100 x 82 cm

Material:

Prepared linen, oil paint
Title is in the Simalyrē language written with the latin alphabet. This work is part of a mythology of a folk having to move out of their homeplace because of war and then being trapped and killed by a mass fire. This mythology is from the world Ardyn Najlyn.
