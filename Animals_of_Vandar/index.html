Name:

Blue-eyed fly (Oculis azurii)

Date:

June 2024

Size:

3x1,5x2cm
The Oculis azurii, named the Blue-eyed fly in the common tongue, is part of the diptera order. Not much is known about this species as its origins are not from earth. Research is being done to discover more.

Name:

Horned damselfly

Date:

May 2024
The horned damselfly is shorter than the damselflies we see on earth. Little is known about this creature as its origins are not from earth. Research is being done to discover more.

Bleedinggrass

Date:

June 2024
The Bleedinggrass seems to be part of the oligochaeta, the class of worms, and gets its name from the bright red front part that looks like it was dipped in blood. Not much is known about this species as its origins are not from earth. Research is being done to discover more.

Name:

Diamondweaver

Date:

June 2024
The Diamondweaver is an unknown exopterygota with yellow wings and coloured body. The name comes from the diamond shape on the body. Not much is known about this species as its origins are not from earth. Research is being done to discover more.

Name:

Grassgulper (Herbivora magnum)

Date:

June 2024

Size:

2x1x1,5 cm
The Grassgulper is one of the smallest mammals ever found and it doesn’t originate from earth. Where it does come from is still unknown and researchers are hoping to discover more after researching this curious creature fully. The creature stands on four strong legs, holding a round torso out of which a sort of grass has grown. The head consists of a large bottom jaw, of which it gets its name ‘gulper’, and two eyes that stick out of its head half a centimetre.

Name:

Kingshieldcarrier

Date:

June 2024

Size:

5x1,5x1 cm
This crab-like creature either has lost a pair of legs or is a class of animal not known by researchers. The Kingshieldcarrier has a wide, thin, strong body being carried by six legs and has a belly made of jewel-like stones. This is where the name comes from. It has a pair of white pearl eyes on the front and antennae above them. It is unknown where this creature comes from, how it lives and how it got here. Research is being done to discover more.
